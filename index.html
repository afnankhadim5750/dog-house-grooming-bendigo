What we do

Services, done properly

Every appointment is one-on-one time with your dog — no rushing, no shortcuts. Choose the groom that suits your dog's coat, age and lifestyle.

Most loved
01

The Signature Full Groom

Our complete, unhurried groom from nose to tail. We shape the coat to suit your dog's breed and your lifestyle — practical for muddy paws, polished for the show ring, or somewhere happily in between.

  • Warm bath & fluff dry
  • Breed-specific clip & scissor
  • Nails, ears & finishing touches
All the details
02

Bath & Blow-Dry

For the in-between weeks. A warm, thorough bath with a gentle rinse, followed by a careful fluff dry that leaves the coat soft, clean and smelling wonderful — perfect maintenance between full grooms.

  • Warm gentle bath
  • Thorough rinse & fluff dry
  • Brush-out & de-tangle
All the details
03

Tidy-Ups & Finishing Touches

Face, feet and fanny trims, nail clips, ear cleans — the little jobs that keep your dog comfortable and looking sharp. Quick visits, calmly handled, ideal between full grooms.

  • Face, feet & hygiene trim
  • Nail trim & file
  • Ear clean & tidy
All the details
Gentle starts
04

Puppy's First Groom

A slow, happy introduction to the salon for young dogs. We let puppies explore at their own pace — a little bath, a little dry, lots of praise — so grooming becomes something they look forward to for life.

  • Unhurried introduction
  • Gentle bath & dry
  • Lots of praise & patience

How it works

From hello to handsome, in four steps

A calm, consistent ritual — the same careful sequence for every dog, every visit.

1

Consult

We start with a chat about your dog's coat, lifestyle and the look you love — then agree on a plan together.

2

Bath

A warm, gentle bath with a thorough rinse, setting the coat up perfectly for the styling to come.

3

Style

Careful clipping and scissoring, shaped to your dog's breed and body — never rushed, always precise.

4

Finish

Nails, ears and those final flourishes — finished with a bow and a very proud parade home.
