Our services

Grooming, tailored to your dog

No two coats are the same, so no two grooms are either. Tell us about your dog and we'll recommend the right service — then shape it around them.

Most loved
01

The Signature Full Groom

Our complete nose-to-tail groom. After a consultation about the look you want, your dog enjoys a warm bath, a careful fluff dry, then breed-specific clipping and scissoring shaped to their body. We finish with nails, ears and those final flourishes.

  • Consultation first
  • Warm bath & fluff dry
  • Breed-specific clip & scissor
  • Nails, ears & finishing touches
Book a full groom
02

Bath & Blow-Dry

The perfect in-between groom. A warm, thorough bath with a gentle rinse to keep skin happy, followed by a careful fluff dry and brush-out. Your dog goes home soft, clean and smelling wonderful — no clipping involved.

  • Warm gentle bath
  • Thorough rinse
  • Fluff dry & brush-out
  • De-tangling as needed
Book a bath
03

Tidy-Ups & Finishing Touches

The little jobs that make a big difference. Face, feet and hygiene trims to keep things neat, nail trims for comfortable paws, and ear cleans — quick, calm visits that slot neatly between full grooms.

  • Face, feet & hygiene trim
  • Nail trim & file
  • Ear clean & tidy
Book a tidy-up
Gentle starts
04

Puppy's First Groom

A slow, happy first visit designed to make the salon a favourite place. Puppies explore at their own pace with a gentle bath, a little drying, and plenty of praise — building confidence that lasts a lifetime of grooms.

  • Unhurried introduction
  • Gentle bath & dry
  • Praise & patience throughout
Book a puppy groom
